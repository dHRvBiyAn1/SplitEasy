# SplitEasy shared expenses

People share expenses and record repayments while retaining the history of who paid and who owed each share.

## Language

**Account**: A sign-in identity that can act in SplitEasy and hold group membership. An account can own more than one financial participant.
_Avoid_: financial participant when referring to the person signing in.

**Financial participant**: A lasting identity to which paid amounts, owed shares and repayments belong. A participant can exist before its owner has an account.
_Avoid_: account or user when referring only to a party in a financial record.

**Group membership**: An account's current permission to access and act in a group. Having financial history in the group does not itself grant membership.

**Financial roster**: The participants currently eligible for new financial entries in a group, including participants without accounts.

**Participant claim**: A verified, explicit acceptance that links a previously unclaimed participant's financial records to an account.

**Net balance**: The amount a participant is owed overall or owes overall in one currency. A zero net balance can still coexist with direct obligations to other people.

**Direct obligation**: An amount one person owes another after their reciprocal recorded obligations are offset in the same currency.

**Simplified obligation**: An amount selected from the group's net balances to settle its debts in one currency. It does not replace the recorded expense and repayment history.
